# District Heating System Controls & Thermal Efficiency Modelling 🌡️

**Associated with:** Åland University of Applied Sciences  
**Domain:** Thermal Systems, Control Systems, Sustainable Energy, Smart Grid Integration  
**Tools Used:** MATLAB, Simulink, Thermal Analysis  

---

## 📌 Project Overview
District heating networks in Finland require precise dynamic thermal modeling to maintain distribution efficiency during extreme winter conditions. This project focuses on modeling heat exchanger dynamic responses and designing control loops to optimize heat distribution and dynamic thermal load management.

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Technical Scope
- **Thermal Dynamic Modeling:** Modeled dynamic responses of heat exchangers under varying ambient Nordic temperatures.
- **Control Loop Design:** Designed and simulated closed-loop feedback controllers (PI/PID) in Simulink to stabilize secondary loop supply temperatures.
- **Efficiency Optimization:** Evaluated mass flow rates and thermal inertia to minimize heat distribution losses during sudden load changes.
